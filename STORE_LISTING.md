# Draft store listing

**Name:** Tasks
**Subtitle / short description (30-80 chars):** Simple to-do lists with priorities and subtasks
**Category:** Productivity
**Price:** Free

**Description**
Tasks is a fast, private to-do list. Make as many lists as you like, add due dates and details, break big jobs into subtasks, and flag what matters with High, Medium and Low priorities.

- Multiple lists plus Starred, Today and Upcoming views
- Priorities, with filters and sorting
- Subtasks, search, and quick due dates
- Repeating tasks (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Light and dark mode
- Works offline. Your tasks stay on your device.
